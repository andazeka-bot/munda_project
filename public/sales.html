Sales

Create better products with textile light

MUNDA is a pure development company and works exclusively for its two parent companies, AUNDE and MENTOR. The textile lighting solutions we develop are available directly from MENTOR or AUNDE.

How it works

Development at MUNDA — manufacturing and sales at our parents

We currently do not provide direct development services for third parties, nor do we manufacture or sell products ourselves. The innovative textile lighting solutions developed at MUNDA — enabling you to integrate light into your products — are available through the sales organisations of AUNDE and MENTOR. We look forward to your enquiry.
