Company

Two worlds. One new technology.

MUNDA Textile Lichtsysteme GmbH in Erkrath was founded by AUNDE and MENTOR as equal partners in the summer of 2019 — combining expertise in technical textiles with decades of LED-based light technology.

The name

MUNDA — from MENTOR and AUNDE

Our name symbolises the basic idea behind the joint venture: two companies with a common objective, each contributing their key expertise to create something new — textile lighting systems — at the highest technical level and suitable for mass production in the most demanding industries.

AUNDE — technical textiles

A globally leading automotive supplier, AUNDE contributes expertise in the field of technical textiles. Founded in Mönchengladbach in 1899, the AUNDE Group produces yarns, technical textiles, foams, seat covers, seats, technical springs and interior modules — with more than 24,500 employees in 117 factories across 28 countries.

www.aunde-group.de ↗

MENTOR — product-integrated light

One of Germany’s leading specialists in product-integrated light, MENTOR brings many years of experience in LED-based light technology. Founded in 1920, MENTOR develops and produces LED-based lighting systems for ambient and functional lighting as well as HMI components — with production sites on three continents and representations in over 40 countries.

www.mentor.de.com ↗

“The technology under development at MUNDA will open up new areas of application and further growth opportunities in many industries.”

— Wido Weyer, Managing Director of MENTOR

The vision

Light as atmosphere

Where previously functional perspectives such as good orientation dominated automotive interior lighting, today emotional aspects — atmosphere, well-being, character — and the desire for individual lighting take centre stage. The mega trend towards autonomous driving will fuse all of these further: dynamic light scenarios will determine the ambience of interior spaces, with virtually no area left out.

“The textile lighting systems from MUNDA will meet these new requirements and help create entirely new customer experiences.”

— Rolf Königs, CEO of the AUNDE Group

And it is not just the automotive industry: demand for product-integrated lighting is rising in many other sectors whose products are becoming increasingly multifunctional — generating practical added value through functional, aesthetic and emotional aspects.
