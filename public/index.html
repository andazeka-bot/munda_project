The idea

Light guides made of fabric

MUNDA combines two worlds: flexible technical textiles and precision LED light technology. The result is a light guide that is less than 5 mm thin, folds into almost any shape, and can be draped into free-form surfaces — where rigid plastic light guides reach their limits.

01

Large areas

Ideal for illuminating large surfaces evenly and homogeneously.

02

Free-form shapes

Folds into (almost) any shape — new design freedom in non-textile products too.

03

Dynamic light

Individual fibres assigned to separate LEDs create fascinating dynamic scenarios.

04

Easy assembly

Simple to assemble and drape — greater flexibility in complicated installation spaces.

World premiere · Summer 2024

The Audi A3 Facelift — first production vehicle with MUNDA textile lighting

Audi is the first car manufacturer in the world to integrate our textile lighting system into the door panels of a series-production vehicle — impressively demonstrating how this innovation makes large-scale ambient lighting possible aesthetically and safely, even in crash-sensitive zones.

  • Large-area ambient lighting in crash-sensitive door zones
  • Less than 5 mm installation height, freely formable
  • Dynamic light scenarios via individually addressable fibre bundles
Why textile light

Relevant advantages over other technologies

01

Optimal use of installation space

The light guide needs less than 5 mm for its entire design and can be folded into almost any shape. The installation space of the light guide is flexibly separated from the electronics — enabling system integration with minimal changes to existing products and production processes, including facelifts and relaunches.

02

Low initial investment and unit costs

Unlike injection moulding, textile lighting involves no component-specific tool investment — making it extremely cost-effective for small-scale production. And because tool costs rise exponentially with light guide size, textile light guides become even more economical the larger the illuminated area gets.

03

Short development times — fast prototyping

As a roll-to-roll textile product, the light guide is almost a carry-over part: process parameters are adapted for each customer application. Tool-free technology plus available LED modules enable extremely short development times and early, series-ready visualisation — and design changes at a late stage become easier or even possible for the first time.

04

Dynamic light

Assign individual fibres or fibre bundles to different LED modules and you create dynamic lighting scenarios that other technologies achieve only with great effort — or not at all.

05

Crash test — no problem

The flexibility of textile lighting systems lets them be integrated even in crash-sensitive zones of a vehicle — illuminating larger interior surfaces than would be permissible with other technologies.
